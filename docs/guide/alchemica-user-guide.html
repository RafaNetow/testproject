Part A

For clients

Everything you need to follow your engagement, without the technical detail.

A1

Signing in

There is no password to remember. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your work email.

  1. Go to the portal sign-in page and enter your email address.
  2. Select Send magic link. A message titled "Your sign-in link" arrives within a minute.
  3. Open the link on the same device and select Finish signing in. This extra click protects you from email apps that open links automatically.
Tip. If the link has expired or was opened in another browser, simply request a new one.
A2

Your Executive Summary

The first page you see answers two questions: where are we? and what do you need from me?

Waiting on you

One list with everything that needs your action, most urgent first. Each row has a single button:

ButtonWhat it means
ReviewA deliverable is ready. Approve it or ask for changes.
AnswerWe need your input on a question on the board.
UploadWe need data from you (for example oven logs or FTIR exports).
SignA contract is waiting for your signature.
OpenAxel sent you a message.

Items past their date are marked Overdue.

A3

Progress, stages and the timeline

Each project is split into stages (for example Onboarding, Preliminary experiments, Main experiments, Final report). The large percentage is the project's overall progress; each stage shows its own.

The timeline places the stages on a calendar. The red line is today; diamonds mark meetings and deliverable dates. Select a stage to open it:

  • On time? On track, "About 3 days behind", "Starts in 5 days", "2 days overdue" or "Done" — calculated from the dates and the percentage.
  • What happens — a plain-language description written by Axel.
  • What falls in it — deliverables, meetings and data requests dated inside the stage, each with its status and a link.
A4

Files and data requests

All project files live in folders. Each file carries a label for the technique or document type (NMR, FTIR, DSC, Process log, Report…). Hover a label to see what it means in plain words.

Sending requested data

  1. Find the item under Requested data (it also appears under Waiting on you).
  2. Choose the file and select Send. It is saved in the right folder with the right label.
  3. The item turns Received and Alchemica is notified.
A5

Reviewing deliverables

Reports and proposals arrive in Deliverables. When one is Ready for review, open it and choose:

  • Approve — you accept it as delivered.
  • Request changes — write what should change; Alchemica is notified and sends a revised version.
A6

The board

The board shows the work as cards in columns. The column Waiting on you holds questions only you can answer. Open a card to read the details, comment and attach files.

Keyboard. Press Tab to reach a card and Enter to open it.
A7

Messages with Axel

Select the chat icon in the top bar (next to the bell) from any page. A panel opens on the right with your project's conversation; the page behind stays where it was.

  • Press Enter to send, Shift + Enter for a new line. Use the paperclip to attach a file — it is also saved in Files › Chat attachments.
  • "Seen by Axel" appears under your message once he has read it. Axel usually replies within one business day.
  • Agreements made in the chat are logged as decisions or added to the board by Alchemica, so nothing gets lost.
A8

Notifications and the weekly email

The bell shows what changed: new files, deliverables, decisions, messages. Select a notification to go straight to it. New items also appear as a short pop-up while you are using the portal.

Every Monday morning you receive a short email: what's waiting on you, where each project stands, and what happened last week. If there is nothing to report, no email is sent.

A9

Choosing a colour

Select the palette icon in the top bar to choose the portal's accent colour. It only changes your own view on that browser.

Part B

For the Alchemica team

Running each engagement from the admin, and keeping the client's view clear.

B1

Dashboard and client pages

The Dashboard summarises active engagements and the next meeting. Clients lists each organisation; open one to see its projects.

Settings. The gear icon on a client page holds organisation details, team invitations and the delete option.
B2

Stages, progress and descriptions

Open Progress & stages under a project. For each stage you can set:

  • The name and the percentage (slider or number). The project total is the average of its stages.
  • Start and end dates — these place the stage on the client's timeline and drive the "On track / behind" check.
  • What happens in this stage — one or two plain sentences the client reads when they open the stage.

Changes save when you leave the field, and the client is notified (for example "completed the stage …").

B3

Requesting data

In a project's Files, use Request data to list what you need: a title, details, the technique and the folder it should land in, and a due date.

When data arrives by email instead, mark the request received yourself; Reopen undoes it, and the bin withdraws a request.

B4

Deliverables

Create a deliverable as a draft (the client cannot see drafts), attach the file, then move it to Ready for review. The client approves it or requests changes; you are notified either way.

B5

Files: labels and folders

Uploads are labelled automatically from the filename. To change a label, select the label chip on the file's row and pick from the list — each option shows the plain-language explanation the client will see. Files without a label show Add label.

To move a file, open the row's ⋯ menu and choose a folder under Move to folder.

B6

Messages: the client list

The chat icon in the top bar shows the number of unread messages. It opens the list of all client conversations, unread first; select one to reply in the same panel and use the arrow to go back.

Open a message's ⋮ menu to Log as decision (it goes to the decisions log) or Add to board (a new card in the first column). A short note in the chat records it for both sides.

B7

Weekly digest preview

Each client member receives a Monday email unless they turned it off. Open /admin/digest-preview to see exactly what any member would receive this week — nothing is sent from that page.

B8

Colours and good habits

The palette icon lets you compare the five accent colours on your own browser; clients choose theirs independently.

Good habits that keep the client's view clear

  • Give every stage dates and a one-line description — the timeline then explains itself.
  • Use data requests instead of emails for anything you need from the client.
  • When something is agreed in the chat, log it as a decision straight away.
  • Keep deliverables as drafts until they are ready; move them to review in one step.
  • Check the digest preview on Monday morning if a client has had a busy week.
