## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: R&D and formulation leads at companies building bio-based, polymeric and industrial materials (adhesives, polymer blends, coatings). They arrive with a concrete, costly problem — failed or inconsistent batches, results they can't explain — and are evaluating whether to bring in a senior chemistry advisor.

Secondary (authenticated): existing clients using the client portal to follow their engagement, and the Alchemica admin running engagements.

## Product Purpose

Alchemica is a chemistry and materials advisory. It turns a client's existing formulation records and characterisation data (NMR, FTIR, TGA, DSC) into a written root cause and a prioritised next experiment, so fewer batches get wasted. Success for the public site: a qualified R&D lead understands the offer quickly and starts a conversation via the contact form or email.

## Positioning

Senior interpretation, not testing: Alchemica does not run the lab work, it reads the data the client already has and says what to try next. Each engagement is a single structured review with a clear deliverable, not an open-ended retainer. Founder-led.

## Operating Context

- Engagements run through a dedicated client portal: board, decisions log, files, meetings, hours, invoices, contracts.
- Clients are in Denmark/Europe; invoicing follows Danish rules (CVR, VAT).
- Contact entry points: the homepage contact form and hello@alchemica.dk.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the client's problem (wasted batches, unexplained results), not with the firm.
2. Concrete over generic: name the techniques, the failure modes and the deliverable.
3. Honest evidence: illustrative scenarios are labelled as such; nothing invented.
4. One clear next step for the visitor: start a conversation.
